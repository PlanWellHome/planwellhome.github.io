Small Business Bookkeeping

Simple bookkeeping for freelancers, makers and small shops: log income, expenses and miles, and get a monthly P&L and a tax summary for your accountant.

What's inside

  • Income and expense logs: every expense gets its Schedule C line from its category
  • Mileage log: miles or odometer, round trips, deduction at your rate
  • Monthly profit & loss for the whole tax year
  • Tax summary by Schedule C line + quarterly set-aside with due dates
  • Dashboard: profit, margin, miles, set-aside + 4 charts
  • A full example year for a small studio
Spreadsheet for Excel and Google Sheets, with a filled-in example and a step-by-step guide.

How it works

  1. Type your tax year and mileage rate.
  2. Log income and expenses as they happen.
  3. Log business drives on the mileage tab.
  4. Hand the tax summary to your accountant.
