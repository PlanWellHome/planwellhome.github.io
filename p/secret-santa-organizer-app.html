What's inside

  • Fair random draw: nobody gets themselves, every rule is kept, every match equally likely
  • Rules: households, "can't draw" pairs and last year's pairs are skipped
  • Keep it secret from the organizer too: everyone reveals their own match
  • Wishlists, sizes and "please no" notes on every reveal
  • Print fold-over reveal cards, or copy a private message for each person
  • Gift tracker: bought, wrapped, given, and spending vs. the budget
Offline app that opens in your web browser. No account, nothing to install.

How it works

  1. Download the .html file and double-click it to open in your browser.
  2. Add everyone, their household and wishlist.
  3. Set your rules and press Draw names.
  4. Each person reveals their match, or print the cards.
