What's inside

  • Weigh-in log: change, total lost, 7-weigh-in average and BMI for every line
  • Your pace over the last 4 weeks and the date you'll reach your goal at that pace
  • Milestones every 5 lb (or 2 kg), with the date reached and your reward
  • Monthly body measurements: waist, hips, chest, arm and thigh
  • Dashboard: 12 tiles, lost so far, to go, % of goal + 4 charts
  • Pounds and inches or kilos and centimeters · filled-in example included
Spreadsheet for Excel and Google Sheets, with a filled-in example and a step-by-step guide.

How it works

  1. Pick lb or kg and type your start and goal weight.
  2. Add a line each time you weigh in.
  3. Add your measurements once a month.
  4. Watch your pace, milestones and goal date.
