What's inside

  • Materials library: type what a pack costs and how many are in it, get the cost per unit
  • A recipe for every product: materials, minutes of work, packaging and shipping
  • Seller fee presets for Etsy sellers, Shopify and craft fairs (card reader), or type your own
  • Profit and margin for every product, and the lowest .99 price that reaches your target margin
  • Price checker: your profit during a sale or coupon, at wholesale, and the sales you need for your monthly goal
  • Charts that show where each dollar goes, plus a printable price list
Offline app that opens in your web browser. No account, nothing to install.

How it works

  1. Download the .html file and double-click it to open in your browser.
  2. Pick where you sell and type your hourly rate and target margin.
  3. Add your materials, then each product's recipe.
  4. Read the profit and the suggested price, and check a sale before you run it.
